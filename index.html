Play Library

Saved plays are kept online in your account, so they are the same on every device you sign in on. Click a play to open it in the Builder. Use Save as Play under the frame description in the Builder to save or update one; its tags save straight away. Search by name or tag, or click a tag above the list to filter. Import file… adds only plays. Share Online sends a copy straight to another approved coach. Share File sends a play as a file they load with Import file…. Copy as Drill saves a copy in the Drill Library and leaves the play here.

Drill Library

Draw a drill in the Builder, then press Save as Drill under the frame description. A drawing is kept in one library only, so saving a drill as a play (or the reverse) moves it. Drills are not used in playbooks. Import file… adds only drills. Share Online sends a copy straight to another approved coach. Share File sends a drill as a file they load with Import file…. Copy as Play saves a copy in the Play Library and leaves the drill here.

Playbooks

A playbook is built from plays in your Play Library, grouped into sections. The PDF gets a contents page with page numbers. Share Online sends a copy of a playbook, with its plays, to another approved coach. Import file… adds only playbooks, and only ones whose plays are already in your Play Library, so import the plays first.

Training Plans

Session plans saved from the Training Plan Builder appear here, grouped by team and competition. Open one to keep editing it. Combined PDF puts every session in a group into one document with a cover page (team, competition and logo) in date order. Import file… adds only training plans. Share Online sends a copy straight to another approved coach. Share File sends a training plan as a file they load with Import file….

Backup

Export everything downloads a .json file you can keep or email. Import a backup file… puts everything in such a file into your account in one go (plays, drills, training plans, then playbooks). You can also use Import file… in each section, and each of those buttons only imports its own section. Import adds to what you already have and never overwrites a play or drill that differs; it adds a copy instead. Session plans have their own Import file… under Training Plans. The Export… and Backup buttons on each play, drill, playbook or training plan export just that one.

Coaches

Coaches create their own account from the Playboard page address and confirm their email. They then appear here, and nothing is visible to them until you approve them. Blocking someone cuts off their access straight away.

Insert drills
